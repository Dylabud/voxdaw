Roland TR-808 samples via the oramics/sampled collection
(https://github.com/oramics/sampled, DM/TR-808 - originally from the
Music Machines archive, machines.hyperreal.org; distributed as public
domain / open license). Converted wav -> aac/m4a at 192kbps.
Variant mapping (01/03/05... = TR-808 tone/decay settings) in
src/components/Workstation/drumKits.js.
